1600 Eberhardt Rd — Warehouse & Flex Space for Lease in Temple, TX

108,500 SF in Temple Industrial Park, Temple, TX 76504. Divisible to approximately 54,000 SF, with direct access to IH-35 and 997 parking spaces.

Property Overview

1600 Eberhardt Rd is a 108,500 SF flex and industrial facility on 14.26 acres in Temple Industrial Park. The building was constructed in 2001 as industrial tilt wall with steel joist and metal deck, and is protected by a wet sprinkler system throughout. A new TPO roof was installed in July 2026.

The space can be leased whole or divided into two suites of roughly equal size — Suite 100 at 53,817 SF and Suite 150 at 54,683 SF — making it workable for a single large user or two mid-size tenants.

The property sits inside the same industrial park as Meta’s $800M hyperscale data center, alongside ongoing infrastructure investment in the corridor.
