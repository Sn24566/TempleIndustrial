Site Overview

2520 N General Bruce Dr is 32.56 acres of industrial land with direct visibility and frontage access on IH-35, and a BNSF rail line running the full length of the west boundary — roughly 2,040 feet of rail frontage.

The site is zoned for both heavy and light industrial use and already has stabilized areas and driveways in place. It suits industrial outdoor storage, distribution, or manufacturing, and supports up to 400,000 SF of building.

Available for sale, ground lease, or build-to-suit. The gallery includes example layouts prepared by Cerris Builders to show what the site can accommodate — these are illustrative, not a fixed menu.
