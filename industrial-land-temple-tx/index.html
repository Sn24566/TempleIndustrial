Build-to-Suit in Temple

Build-to-suit means we deliver a finished building constructed to your specification, and you lease it, rather than adapting your operation to a building that already exists. It suits users whose requirements — clear height, power, dock configuration, rail spur — are specific enough that existing inventory does not work.

The 2520 N General Bruce Dr page includes example layouts. They are there to show what the site can hold, not to limit the options.

Typical structures

  • Sale — you buy the land and develop it yourself
  • Ground lease — you lease the land long-term and build on it
  • Build-to-suit — we build to your spec and you lease the finished facility

Why Develop in Temple

Temple sits on IH-35 between Austin and Waco, about 45 minutes north of Austin, with roughly 28 million people within a two-and-a-half hour drive.

For a developing user, the practical draw is that Central Texas land and labour costs remain workable while highway and rail access do not have to be traded away. BNSF runs through the industrial corridor, and rail-served acreage that is actually available is scarce in most comparable markets.

The corridor is also attracting significant capital — Meta’s $800M hyperscale data center campus sits inside Temple Industrial Park, directly bordering our Nugent Ave parcel.
