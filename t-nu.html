Site Overview

2606 W Nugent Ave is a 32-acre development-ready site in Temple Industrial Park, with frontage on W Nugent Ave and easy access to IH-35. The parcel supports up to 400,000 SF of warehouse or manufacturing space.

The site is available for sale, ground lease, or build-to-suit, so a user can take the land outright or have us deliver a finished building to their specification.

Directly borders Meta’s $800M hyperscale data center campus, and adjoins our own 108,500 SF facility at 1600 Eberhardt Rd.
